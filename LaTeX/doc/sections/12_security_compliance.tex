\section{Security and Compliance}
\label{sec:security-compliance}

This section summarises the device's security posture and its status under the
European Union Cyber Resilience Act (CRA), Regulation (EU) 2024/2847. Detailed
findings live in the \texttt{sec/} folder of the repository and are referenced below.

\subsection{Security Analysis}

A web interface security analysis was performed on 2026-08-27 and is documented in
\texttt{sec/2026-08-27\_Webinterface\_Sicherheitsanalyse.md}. The browser interface
is the primary control surface for a valve regulating pressures up to approximately
10\,bar. The critical findings are:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Finding} & \textbf{Location} & \textbf{CRA Annex I clause} \\
\midrule
No authentication on control endpoints & \texttt{WebHandlers.cpp} & 1.2 (authentication, access control) \\
Hardcoded, public AP password & \texttt{Constants.h} & 1.1 (no default passwords) \\
Calibration writable via HTTP, defeats shutdown & \texttt{handleSet()} / \texttt{isPressureSafe()} & 1.7 (integrity, safety) \\
State changes via HTTP GET (CSRF) & \texttt{/set}, \texttt{/resetPID} & 1.10 (secure defaults) \\
Arbitrary file read (path traversal) & \texttt{onNotFound()} & 1.4 (attack surface) \\
Unbounded PID gain ranges & \texttt{/api/slider-limits} & 1.7 (input validation) \\
Flash wear via repeated \texttt{save()} & \texttt{/set} handler & 1.3 (availability) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Security findings mapped to CRA essential requirements.}
\end{table}

Two robustness items were fixed in v2.7.3: the settings access mutex (P3.1) and the
client-connect denial-of-service behaviour (P3.2). The remaining items are tracked as
an implementation plan with priorities P1 (critical) through P3 (robustness).

\subsection{EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)}

VentCon2 is a product with digital elements and therefore falls under Regulation
(EU) 2024/2847 once it is placed on the EU market in a commercial activity. It is a
default-category product, so conformity is demonstrated by self-assessment under
Module A, with no notified body required. The applicable obligations are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Security by design and by default (Annex I, Part I).
    \item A documented cybersecurity risk assessment.
    \item A software bill of materials (SBOM) of all components.
    \item A signed over-the-air update mechanism.
    \item A declared support period of at least 5 years, with its end date published.
    \item A vulnerability disclosure policy and a security contact.
    \item CE marking, technical documentation (Annex VII), and an EU declaration of
          conformity (Annex V).
    \item Incident reporting to the national CSIRT and ENISA from 2026-09-11, with
          full application of all obligations from 2027-12-11.
\end{itemize}

Because the device contains Wi-Fi, the Radio Equipment Directive (RED) and Delegated
Regulation (EU) 2022/30 may apply instead of the CRA for the cybersecurity
requirements. This classification must be confirmed before conformity assessment.

\subsection{Compliance Status}

The complete compliance checklist is maintained in
\texttt{sec/2026-08-29\_CRA\_Compliance\_Checklist.md}. In summary, the
security-critical code fixes (Phase 1 and Phase 2 of the security analysis) are open,
and the documentation package (risk assessment, SBOM, support period, technical
documentation, declaration of conformity) has not yet been produced.
