CRA Compliance Addendum (Supplier Agreement)

Addendum to the Supply Agreement —

Compliance with the Cyber Resilience Act (“Regulation”)

This Addendum is entered into between [Aalberts Group Company] (“Company”) and [Supplier] (“Supplier”), and forms an integral part of the existing Supply Agreement between the parties. The obligations set out below are binding on both parties from the date of signature below.

1. General compliance. Supplier represents and warrants that, to the extent applicable to it, it shall comply with all obligations under the Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847) , including essential cybersecurity requirements, vulnerability handling, and reporting obligations, and shall ensure that all software, firmware, or digital elements supplied to Company are designed, developed, and maintained in accordance with that Regulation.

2. Notification. Supplier shall notify Company promptly, and in any event within 24 hours of becoming aware, of any actively exploited vulnerability, security incident, or newly discovered vulnerability affecting any software, firmware, or digital component supplied to Company.

3. SBOM. Supplier shall provide Company with a complete and updated software bill of materials for all supplied components upon request in a machine-readable, commonly used format.

4. Vulnerability disclosure policy. To the extent Supplier maintains a vulnerability disclosure policy or coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy, Supplier shall share such policy with Company no later than 30 days following the execution date of this Addendum, and at any time thereafter promptly upon Company's request.

5. Remediation. Supplier shall acknowledge receipt of any vulnerability report submitted by Company within 48 hours of such receipt. Following such acknowledgement, Supplier shall investigate the reported vulnerability and provide Company with a substantive response, including a remediation plan within 30 days of the acknowledgement, and shall implement a fix or mitigation without undue delay thereafter.

6. Audit right. Upon reasonable notice, Supplier shall permit Company or its designated auditor to audit Supplier’s compliance with the CRA and this Addendum, including documentation, vulnerability handling processes, and incident records. As far as Supplier is unable to extend Supplier’s audit rights to source code, it shall replace such code within a year after the signature date of this CRA Compliance Addendum with code for which it has the right to grant audit rights to Company.

7. Breach. Failure by Supplier to comply with this Addendum constitutes a material breach of the Agreement, entitling Company to seek damages, require remediation at Supplier’s cost, or terminate the Agreement.

8. Precedence. In case of conflict between this Addendum and the Agreement, this Addendum prevails on CRA-related matters.
