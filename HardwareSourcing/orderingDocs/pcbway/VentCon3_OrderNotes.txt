VentCon 3 ordering notes

PCB fabrication
- 4 layers, 55.88 x 90.15 mm, FR-4, 2.8 mm, 1 oz copper.
- HASL lead-free, green solder mask, white silkscreen.
- No impedance control required.
- Non-plated holes: 14 (2 x D3.30, 4 x D2.05, 4 x D1.55, 4 x D1.651 mm),
  all in VentCon3-NPTH.drl. The circular contours of the original outline
  layer were removed, so no additional cut-outs or rout slots are needed.
- Vias D0.35 mm, smallest trace 0.254 mm, smallest annular ring 0.203 mm.
  With 2.8 mm thickness this is an 8:1 aspect ratio, please confirm it is
  within the standard process.

Assembly
- Turnkey, both sides, 15 pcs.
- Do not place: J1, U1, S1, S2, S3. These parts are assembled by the customer
  and are not part of the BOM, 15 sets will be supplied separately.
- H1 and H2 are stand-off pads, not components.
- THT parts C4, C10, IC1, IC2, Q1: please hand-solder (13 joints).
- No substitution for C3 (C0G), C4/C10 (105 degC, low ESR), D1/D2 (SMB,
  not SMC), R7/R9 (0.1 %, 25 ppm/K), IC1, IC2, Q1 (Infineon only), U2.
- F1 and F2 must be rated for at least 32 V. C12 must not be Y5V.
- C2, C12, F1 and F2 have no MPN yet, please select to the specification in
  the BOM and quote.
- U2 (VSSOP-10, 0.5 mm pitch): please keep a solder mask dam of at least
  0.10 mm between the pads.
- Polarity: D1, D2 (cathode), C4, C10 (minus), IC1, IC2, Q1 and U2 are marked
  on the silkscreen.
